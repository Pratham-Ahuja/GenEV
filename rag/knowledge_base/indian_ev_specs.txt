EV SELECTION GUIDE — INDIA
================================================================

FOR DAILY COMMUTE UNDER 50 KM:
- Best: Tata Tiago EV (budget) or Ather 450X (scooter)
- Why: Sufficient range, low cost, easy charging

FOR DAILY COMMUTE 50-100 KM:
- Best: Tata Nexon EV MR or Punch EV
- Why: Comfortable range buffer, fast charging available

FOR INTERCITY TRAVEL:
- Best: Nexon EV LR, BYD Atto 3, Mahindra XEV 9e
- Why: High range, DC fast charging support

FOR APARTMENT DWELLERS (No Home Charging):
- Best: Ather 450X (portable charger), MG Windsor (BaaS)
- Why: Public charging network, flexible battery options

FOR FAMILIES:
- Best: Tata Nexon EV, MG Windsor, BYD Atto 3
- Why: Space, safety, range comfort

FOR PERFORMANCE ENTHUSIASTS:
- Best: Mahindra XEV 9e, Ola S1 Pro
- Why: High power, fast acceleration

GOVERNMENT SUBSIDIES — INDIA 2025
================================================================

FAME II SCHEME:
- Central subsidy: Rs 10,000 per kWh (capped)
- Maximum subsidy: Rs 1.5 lakh for 4-wheelers
- Maximum subsidy: Rs 15,000 for 2-wheelers
- Valid on: ARAI certified EVs only

STATE SUBSIDIES (Examples):
- Delhi: Rs 1.5 lakh additional subsidy
- Maharashtra: Rs 1 lakh subsidy
- Gujarat: Rs 1.5 lakh subsidy
- Tamil Nadu: Road tax exemption

TOTAL SAVINGS POSSIBLE:
- 4-wheeler: Up to Rs 3 lakh off road price
- 2-wheeler: Up to Rs 30,000 off road price
